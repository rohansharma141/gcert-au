ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management System)

ISO 9001:2015 is the international standard for a Quality Management System (QMS), used by organizations of any size to ensure consistent, high-quality products and services. It focuses on risk management, customer satisfaction, and continuous improvement, applicable across all industries. Certification proves compliance with international quality standards.

ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental Management)

ISO 14001:2015 is the internationally recognized standard for Environmental Management Systems (EMS), providing a framework for organizations to improve environmental performance, reduce waste, and ensure regulatory compliance. It uses a Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) approach applicable to any industry, focusing on sustainability, leadership commitment, and proactive risk management.

ISO 45001:2018 (OH&S Management)

ISO 45001 is the international standard for occupational health and safety (OH&S) management systems, providing a framework to reduce work-related injuries, illnesses, and fatalities. It emphasizes leadership, worker participation, hazard identification, and risk management. It is applicable to any organization wanting to enhance safety performance and demonstrate compliance.

ISO 20000-1:2018 (IT Service Management)

ISO/IEC 20000 is the international standard for IT Service Management (ITSM), providing requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving a Service Management System (SMS). It ensures organizations deliver high-quality, reliable, and efficient IT services, often aligning with ITIL best practices.

ISO 27001:2022 (ISMS)

ISO/IEC 27001 is the international standard for information security management systems (ISMS), providing a risk-based framework to protect data confidentiality, integrity, and availability. It helps organizations identify, manage, and mitigate risks, securing assets like intellectual property and employee data.

ISO 22301:2019 (Business Continuity)

ISO 22301:2019 is the international standard for Business Continuity Management Systems (BCMS), specifying requirements to plan, establish, implement, operate, monitor, and maintain a documented system to protect against, reduce the likelihood of, and recover from disruptive incidents. It helps organizations ensure continuity of operations, enhance resilience, and minimize downtime.

ISO 22000:2018 (Food Safety Management)

ISO 22000 is an international standard for Food Safety Management Systems (FSMS) designed to ensure safety across the entire food chain. It combines HACCP principles, prerequisite programs (PRPs), and management system requirements to help organizations identify, control, and manage food safety hazards.

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (Artificial Intelligence)

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the first international standard for an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS), to provide a framework for responsible AI governance, risk management, and compliance. It helps organizations develop or use AI systems ethically and transparently, ensuring accountability, mitigating risks like bias, and enabling third-party certification.

ISO 13485:2016 (Medical Devices)

ISO 13485 is the internationally recognized standard for quality management systems in the design and manufacture of medical devices. It outlines specific requirements that help organizations ensure their medical devices meet both customer and regulatory demands for safety and efficacy.

SOC 1 and SOC 2 Reports

SOC reports provide assurance regarding information security management systems (ISMS), using a risk-based framework to protect data confidentiality, integrity, and availability. It helps organizations identify, manage, and mitigate risks, securing assets like intellectual property and employee data.

VAPT (Security Assessment)

Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing or VAPT is a cybersecurity approach that identifies, evaluates, and mitigates risks across systems, networks, and applications.

Vulnerability Assessment (VA): Identifies and assesses known vulnerabilities without exploitation.
Penetration Testing (PT): Simulates real-world attacks to exploit vulnerabilities and test security controls. VAPT helps organisations proactively address weaknesses and strengthen their overall cybersecurity posture.

PCI-DSS Compliance

PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) is a mandatory global security standard for any entity that stores, processes, or transmits credit/debit card data. It consists of 12 requirements designed to secure cardholder information, reduce fraud, and prevent breaches. Compliance involves regular assessments, network scanning, and potential audits.

CMMC & CMMI

CMMC: The Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) 2.0 is a US DoD framework designed to enforce security standards for contractors handling sensitive information. It streamlines requirements into three tiers requiring assessment to protect the defense supply chain.

CMMI: Capability Maturity Model Integration is a globally accepted framework to improve organizational processes, capability, and performance. It enables organizations to build, measure, and optimize capabilities, resulting in higher quality products and services. CMMI uses maturity levels (1-5) or capability levels (0-3) to assess improvement.
