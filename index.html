Our Certification Journey

We follow a transparent and rigorous process to ensure your organisation meets all international requirements for certification.

1

Initial Enquiry, Application and Scope Assessment

Submit an enquiry via phone or online to initiate your certification journey, where we assess your application and define the scope.

2

Quotation & Contract

Based on the agreed scope, we provide a fixed-price quote and formalise the certification agreement outlining standards and audit schedules.

3

Stage 1: Documentation Review

A structured review of your management system documentation to confirm readiness for Stage 2, identifying any gaps in implementation.

4

Stage 2: Implementation Audit

On-site assessment of your system’s effectiveness through interviews and record reviews to ensure full compliance.

5

Certification Decision

Findings are independently reviewed before issuing an accredited certificate, valid for three years subject to ongoing surveillance.

6

Surveillance & Recertification

Annual surveillance audits ensure ongoing compliance, followed by a recertification audit every three years to renew your certificate.
